(01) The brief

Owners of service businesses are busy. Get to the point.

An HVAC owner checking an agency site on a phone between jobs needs three answers fast: what you do, whether it works, and how to start. Everything else is noise.

(02) What we built

Proof first, then the ask.

  • A live-looking lead panel in the hero, so the value shows before scrolling
  • A case study with results, clearly labelled as illustrative demo data
  • A three-step process and an FAQ answering the objections owners raise most
  • An enquiry form with inline, screen-reader friendly errors
  • A full mobile menu that keeps every section one tap away

(03) Checks before launch

Nothing ships until it passes.

TypeScript, lint and production build all passing; layouts checked at 375px and 1440px; keyboard focus visible on every control; and every illustrative number clearly marked, because we never present demo data as real results.
