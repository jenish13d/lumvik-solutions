How Northpeak was built

A look at the decisions behind the concept site, the way we'd document a client project for you.

The brief we set ourselves

A local-services marketing agency in Austin needs a site that makes owners of HVAC and plumbing businesses pick up the phone. One page, clear proof, an easy enquiry.

Key decisions

  • Lead panel in the hero, so the value is visible before scrolling
  • Results and testimonial clearly marked as illustrative
  • FAQ answers the three objections owners raise most

Checks before launch

  • TypeScript, lint and production build all passing
  • No horizontal scrolling at 375px and 1280px
  • Form errors announced to screen readers

Stack

  • Next.js and TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Vercel hosting
