How a project runs

Five steps, the same every time. You always know what's happening and what it costs.

  1. Brief

    Send the design file, copy and any must-haves. Figma, Adobe XD, a PDF or a sketch all work.

  2. Quote

    We confirm scope, price and a delivery date in writing before any work starts.

  3. Build

    We build in Next.js or clean HTML, with progress shared on a private preview link.

  4. Review

    You check it on desktop and phone. Two rounds of changes are included.

  5. Handover

    We deploy to your hosting or hand over the code. It's yours, unbranded.

Questions agencies ask

More detail on the agency page.

Will my client know you built it?

No. We never contact your client, never add our name to the site or code, and are happy to sign your NDA.

Who owns the code?

You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. We hand over the repository or deploy straight to your hosting.

How do payments work?

We invoice in US dollars: 50% to start and 50% on handover. Larger projects can be split into milestones.

What if something breaks after launch?

Every build includes 14 days of free fixes for anything that doesn't match the agreed scope.
