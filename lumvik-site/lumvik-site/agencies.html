What you can count on

The basics every agency asks about before trusting an outside developer.

Confidentiality

  • We never contact your client unless you ask us to
  • No Lumvik credits in the site, code or files
  • We'll sign your NDA, or use ours

Ownership

  • All code and assets are yours once paid
  • Delivered to your GitHub, hosting or as a zip
  • No lock-in, no ongoing license fees

Communication

  • One point of contact, who also writes the code
  • Written updates at the end of every working day
  • Email or Slack, whichever your team uses

Quality checks

  • Tested at phone and desktop widths before you see it
  • Accessible markup, keyboard support and readable contrast
  • Page titles, descriptions and basic SEO in place

From brief to handover

The same five steps for a single landing page or a ten-page site.

  1. Brief

    Send the design file, copy and any must-haves. Figma, Adobe XD, a PDF or a sketch all work.

  2. Quote

    We confirm scope, price and a delivery date in writing before any work starts.

  3. Build

    We build in Next.js or clean HTML, with progress shared on a private preview link.

  4. Review

    You check it on desktop and phone. Two rounds of changes are included.

  5. Handover

    We deploy to your hosting or hand over the code. It's yours, unbranded.

Good fit, not a fit

Being clear about this saves both of us time.

We're a good fit for

  • Agencies with more design work than development hours
  • Landing pages and marketing sites from a finished design
  • Teams that want to try a partner with one small project first

We're not the right fit for

  • Large e-commerce builds with complex inventory
  • Enterprise systems with strict compliance needs
  • Projects that need someone on-site

Partnership questions

Anything else, just ask.

Can we start with a small test project?

Yes, and we recommend it. Send one landing page; if the quality and communication work for you, we take it from there.

What do you need from us to start?

A design file or reference, the copy, any brand assets, and where the site should live. If something's missing, we'll tell you what in the quote.

Which tools and platforms do you build with?

Mostly Next.js, React and Tailwind, deployed on Vercel. We can also deliver clean static HTML. We don't currently build on WordPress or Shopify.

How are changes handled?

Two review rounds are included in every quote. New features beyond the agreed scope are quoted separately before we start them.

How do we pay?

By international bank transfer or PayPal, invoiced in US dollars. 50% upfront, 50% on handover.
