Working together

Who does the work?

A small team: Jay, our founder, leads every project, working with engineers from NIT Rourkela and IIT Goa and developers in the US. Your project has one named lead from brief to handover. We use modern tools, including AI-assisted coding, to move faster, and every change is written or reviewed by an engineer before you see it.

Will my client know you built it?

No. We never contact your client, never add our name to the site or code, and are happy to sign your NDA.

Can we start with a small test project?

Yes, and we recommend it. Send one landing page and judge the quality and communication yourself.

How do we communicate?

By email or Slack, whichever your team uses. You get a written update at the end of every working day.

What hours do you work?

Indian working hours, which run through the US evening and night. Send feedback at the end of your day and progress is usually waiting in the morning.

Pricing and payment

How is a project priced?

From the brief. We send a fixed quote with a delivery date, and that number doesn't change unless the scope does. Use the cost estimator for a quick range.

How do we pay?

Invoiced in US dollars, by international bank transfer or PayPal. 50% to start and 50% on handover.

What isn't included?

Domains, hosting fees, paid plugins or fonts, copywriting, new design work, and maintenance after the 14-day fix period, unless agreed in the quote.

Code and quality

Who owns the code?

You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. We hand over the repository or deploy to your hosting.

What do you build with?

Mostly Next.js, React and Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel, or clean static HTML. We don't currently build on WordPress or Shopify.

How do you check quality?

Every build is tested at phone and desktop widths, checked for keyboard access and readable contrast, and run through our launch checklist before you see it.

What if something breaks after launch?

Every build includes 14 days of free fixes for anything that doesn't match the agreed scope.
