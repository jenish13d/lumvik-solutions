How a project runs.

  1. Brief

    Send the design file, copy and any must-haves. Figma, a PDF or a rough sketch all work.

  2. Quote

    Scope, price and a delivery date agreed in writing before any work starts.

  3. Build

    Built in Next.js or clean HTML, with progress on a private preview link.

  4. Review

    You check it on desktop and phone. Two rounds of changes are included.

  5. Handover

    Deployed to your hosting or handed over as code. Yours, unbranded.

QuestionsBefore you ask

What agencies ask us.

Will my client know you built it?

No. We never contact your client, never add our name to the site or code, and are happy to sign your NDA.

Who owns the code?

You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. We hand over the repository or deploy straight to your hosting.

How do payments work?

We invoice in US dollars: 50% to start and 50% on handover. Larger projects can be split into milestones.

What if something breaks after launch?

Every build includes 14 days of free fixes for anything that doesn't match the agreed scope.

Can we try one small project first?

Yes, and we recommend it. Start with a single landing page and judge the quality and communication for yourself.

Who does the work?

Jay, our founder, leads every project, working with engineers from NIT Rourkela and IIT Goa and developers in the US. One named lead stays with your project from brief to handover.

How fast do you reply?

Within one working day, and usually much faster. Quotes for most briefs arrive within one working day too.

What do you build with?

Mostly Next.js, React and Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel, or clean static HTML when that suits the project better. We don't currently build on WordPress or Shopify.
