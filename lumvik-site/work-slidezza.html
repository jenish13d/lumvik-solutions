(01) The problem

Hours of formatting, and numbers nobody checked.

People spend more time arranging slides than thinking about what to say. AI tools speed that up but tend to write a whole deck in one go, so a bad structure gets baked in, and figures appear with no source.

(02) What we built

Outline first, then the deck, card by card.

  • Prompt to outline: users rename, reorder, add or cut cards before anything is written
  • Cards generate three at a time and appear live; reloading resumes unfinished cards
  • Seven layouts chosen per card, with switchable themes and full-screen present mode
  • Edit any card, or regenerate it with instructions
  • View-only share links and PDF export, one 16:9 card per page
  • Accounts, monthly credits, quality modes, and Stripe Checkout, portal and webhooks

(03) The stack

Small, typed and tested.

Next.js and TypeScript on the front and back end, Prisma for data, Zod for validated AI output, the Claude API for writing, Stripe for billing and Vitest for tests. Credit charges are atomic, so parallel requests can't overspend.
